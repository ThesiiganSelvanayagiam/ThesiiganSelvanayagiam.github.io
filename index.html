What is a SOC Analyst?

A Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst is a cybersecurity professional who works within a dedicated team to monitor, detect, investigate, and respond to security threats in real time. The SOC operates as an organization's first line of defense — a 24/7 command center where analysts watch over the entire digital infrastructure.

SOC Analysts are categorized into tiers: Tier 1 handles alert triage and initial investigation; Tier 2 performs deeper incident analysis and threat hunting; and Tier 3 involves senior threat intelligence and malware analysis.

In the era of escalating ransomware, nation-state attacks, and supply chain vulnerabilities, the SOC Analyst role has become one of the most critical and in-demand positions in modern cybersecurity. Every enterprise — from banking to healthcare — relies on a SOC to protect its digital assets.

Key Job Responsibilities

  • Monitor SIEM dashboards for real-time alerts and anomalies
  • Triage and investigate security incidents (phishing, malware, intrusions)
  • Analyze log data from firewalls, endpoints, and network devices
  • Perform threat hunting using IOCs (Indicators of Compromise)
  • Document incidents and escalate to Tier 2/3 when necessary
  • Conduct vulnerability assessments and patch verification
  • Maintain playbooks and standard operating procedures
  • Coordinate with IT teams during active incident response

Technical Skills Required

  • SIEM tools: Splunk, IBM QRadar, Microsoft Sentinel
  • Network traffic analysis: Wireshark, Zeek, Suricata
  • Endpoint detection: CrowdStrike, Carbon Black
  • Threat intelligence platforms: MISP, VirusTotal
  • Scripting for automation: Python, Bash, PowerShell
  • Understanding of MITRE ATT&CK framework
  • Familiarity with firewall, IDS/IPS configurations
  • Knowledge of TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP protocols

Relevance to Modern Cybersecurity

  • Cybercrime damages projected to exceed USD $10 trillion globally by 2025
  • Average time to detect a breach is still 207 days without a SOC
  • Malaysia's National Cybersecurity Agency (NACSA) reports a 40% surge in incidents yearly
  • SOC roles are among the fastest growing IT careers in Southeast Asia

Career Pathways

  • SOC Analyst Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3
  • Threat Intelligence Analyst
  • Incident Response Specialist
  • Security Engineer / Architect
  • Red Team / Penetration Tester
  • Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

Typical Salary (Malaysia)

  • Fresh Graduate: MYR 3,000–4,500/mo
  • Mid-level (3–5 yrs): MYR 6,000–9,000/mo
  • Senior SOC Analyst: MYR 10,000–15,000/mo
  • SOC Team Lead: MYR 12,000–18,000/mo
  • Strong demand from banking, telco, government sectors
