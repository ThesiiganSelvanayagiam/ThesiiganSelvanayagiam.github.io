💭 Why Maybank? ▾
Maybank is not just an employer — it is the institution that underpins Malaysia's financial sovereignty. As the nation's largest bank with over RM900 billion in assets and a presence in 20+ countries, Maybank's Group Security Operations handles one of the most complex and high-stakes cybersecurity environments in Southeast Asia. The BFSI sector faces the highest volume of targeted cyberattacks globally — and I want to be at that frontline. My studies in network security, IoT encryption, and incident response have been preparing me for exactly this kind of critical infrastructure defense. Maybank's "Humanising Financial Services" mission also resonates deeply: every alert I investigate, every incident I respond to, protects real customers' savings, livelihoods, and financial futures.
💡 What value can I bring to Maybank? ▾
I bring a combination of strong technical foundation, original security research, and proven leadership at scale. My hands-on experience with Wireshark, Metasploit, Autopsy, and cryptographic systems gives me day-one applicable skills for Maybank's SOC. My thesis on Dynamic Hill Cipher + LSB Steganography for IoT healthcare data is directly applicable to Maybank's digital banking security challenges — securing API transmissions, mobile banking data, and fintech integrations. My experience directing IFYPC 2025 with international student delegates demonstrates I can coordinate, communicate, and deliver under pressure — all qualities Maybank's incident response and threat analysis teams need daily.
🎯 Why hire me specifically for Maybank's Security Team? ▾
Three reasons: Specialization — every certification, project, and co-curricular activity was focused on network security. I have been building toward this singular goal for over two years. Academic excellence — a CGPA of 3.9 with 5 consecutive Dean's Lists proves I understand theory deeply, not just surface-level tool usage. Character & mission alignment — I genuinely want to protect Malaysia's financial infrastructure. The banking sector is where cybersecurity matters most to ordinary citizens, and that purpose is what drives me every day.
🔧 What skills make me suitable for Maybank's SOC? ▾
Technically: Wireshark for deep packet inspection of banking traffic, Metasploit & Burp Suite for vulnerability assessment of web banking portals, Autopsy & Ghidra for digital forensics on suspected insider threats, Cisco Packet Tracer & GNS3 for network simulation and security architecture planning. Analytically: Hack@10 International CTF 2026 trained me to investigate, triage, and respond under strict time pressure — closely mirroring the SOC environment Maybank's analysts face. Personally: Bilingual professional communication and extensive leadership experience translate directly into SOC coordination, management reporting, and cross-department escalation workflows within a corporate banking environment.
⚖️ Strengths & Weaknesses? ▾
Strengths: Consistency under pressure — maintaining a CGPA of 3.9 while directing an international conference simultaneously proves I manage complexity without sacrificing quality. Genuine intellectual curiosity — I chose my thesis topic because I found IoT security fascinating, not because it was easy. Weaknesses: Limited enterprise SIEM exposure — I am actively addressing this through Splunk free training and TryHackMe SOC labs, targeting functional proficiency before graduation.
